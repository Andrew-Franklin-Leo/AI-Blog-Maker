## 2026-03-01 - Improving Navigation Perceivability
**Learning:** Using `NavLink` with a visual indicator (like a bottom border) in addition to color changes improves accessibility for users with color vision deficiencies who might not distinguish active/inactive states by text color alone.
**Action:** Always combine color-based active states with secondary visual cues like borders, underlines, or icons.
