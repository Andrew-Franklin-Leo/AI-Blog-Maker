## 2025-05-14 - [Skip to Content Implementation]
**Learning:** Skip to content links should have a high z-index (e.g., z-[60]) to ensure they appear above sticky navbars (e.g., z-50). They also benefit from a solid background and centered positioning when focused to be clearly visible to keyboard users.
**Action:** Always check the z-index of skip links relative to other fixed/sticky elements and use prominent focus styles.

## 2025-05-14 - [Accessible Navigation Indicators]
**Learning:** For users with color vision deficiencies, relying solely on text color for active navigation states is insufficient. Adding a visual cue like a border-bottom (`border-b-2`) provides a clear, non-color-dependent indicator.
**Action:** Always combine color changes with structural or decorative visual cues for active states in navigation elements.
